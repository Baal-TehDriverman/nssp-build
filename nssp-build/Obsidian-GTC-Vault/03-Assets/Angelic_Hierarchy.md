## Overview

The 72 angels of the Shem HaMephorash (Hebrew: שם המפורש, "The Explicit Name") are derived from three verses in Exodus (14:19-21), each containing 72 letters. These angels govern the 72 quinances (5-day periods) of the year and directly oppose the 72 Goetic demons.

Each angel has:
- A name derived from the divine name
- Dominion over specific aspects of creation
- A corresponding Psalm verse for invocation
- An opposing demon (their Goetic counterpart)

## Invocation Mechanics

Invoking an angel requires:
1. **Knowledge** of the angel's name (discovered through lore, quests, or research)
2. **Faith charge** (accumulated through good deeds, resisting temptation, completing divine quests)
3. **Invocational focus** (a holy symbol, relic, or consecrated space)
4. **Timing** aligned with the angel's governing period (45-day window)

Each invocation:
- **Protects** against the angel's opposing demon for 24 hours
- **Grants** the angel's dominion effect (healing, wisdom, etc.)
- **Lasts** until dismissed or the corresponding demon is bound
- **Costs** 1 Soul Coin per invocation (ironic inverse of demon binding)
- **Opposes** binding attempts on the paired demon (50% resistance)

### Divine vs. Infernal: The Balance System

The Shem angels and Goetic demons form a symmetry system:

- **Binding a demon** reduces angelic favor by 1 point
- **Invoking an angel** reduces binding stability by 10%
- **Each angel-demon pair** is locked in opposition
- **90+ demons bound** = Permanent angelic lockout (for the playthrough)
- **Fully angelic path** = Cannot bind any demon, but all angel effects are doubled

The player can choose to pursue either path or walk the middle road, balancing both systems. The True Ending requires maxing one path.
